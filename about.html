Welcome to KINI007

KINI007 is an online marketplace designed to connect sellers, customers, and affiliate marketers through a simple and accessible digital platform. Our goal is to help sellers showcase products and help customers discover products from different sellers.

What We Offer

For Sellers

  • Create a seller account.
  • List and manage products.
  • Choose available membership plans.
  • Manage product information and orders.
  • Participate in affiliate marketing.

For Customers

  • Browse products from different sellers.
  • Discover products and compare available options.
  • Contact sellers regarding products and purchases.

For Affiliates

  • Generate product promotion links.
  • Promote participating products.
  • Track clicks and eligible sales.
  • Receive eligible commissions directly from sellers under the applicable affiliate terms.

Our Marketplace Model

KINI007 operates as a marketplace platform. Product transactions take place directly between customers and sellers. KINI007 does not receive, hold, split, or distribute customer payments for sellers’ product sales.

Sellers are responsible for product pricing, customer payments, delivery, product quality, and applicable refunds. KINI007 receives membership fees from sellers for access to applicable membership features.

Our Mission

To make online selling more accessible by providing a platform where sellers can reach customers and affiliates can help promote products.
