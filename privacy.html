Privacy Policy

How KINI007 collects, uses, and protects information.

Effective Date: 26 November 2026   |   Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect account information such as name, email address, phone number, and account credentials; seller information such as shop name, business address, country, profile details, and identity verification information where required; membership and payment-reference information; and technical information such as browser, device, usage, and security logs.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, operate marketplace services, manage memberships, verify membership payments, prevent fraud, provide support, improve platform performance, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Payment Information

KINI007 does not intend to store raw credit or debit card numbers, CVV codes, card expiry details, passwords, or OTPs. Where payment gateways are used, payment processing will be handled by the relevant payment provider under its own privacy and security policies.

4. Seller Identity Information

Where identity verification is required, sellers may be asked to provide identification information or documents. Such information should only be accessible to authorized personnel for legitimate verification, security, and compliance purposes.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information. Information may be shared with payment providers for membership payment processing, service providers required to operate the platform, authorized personnel handling account or security matters, and government or regulatory authorities when legally required.

6. Customer and Seller Transactions

Product purchases and payments are arranged directly between customers and sellers. KINI007 does not process or hold customer payments for independent sellers’ product sales. Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive financial information through public product listings or messages.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate the platform, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. User Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve functionality, and understand website usage.

11. Third-Party Services

KINI007 may use third-party services such as hosting providers, databases, analytics tools, and payment gateways. Their use of information is governed by their respective policies.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be published on this website.
