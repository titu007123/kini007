Return & Refund Policy

Information about product-sale refunds and KINI007 membership refunds.

Effective Date: 26 November 2026   |   Last Updated: 26 November 2026

1. Product Purchases

Products listed on KINI007 are offered by independent sellers. Customers make product payments directly to the relevant sellers. Sellers are responsible for product delivery, quality and condition, returns, exchanges, refund decisions, refund processing, and resolving product-related disputes.

KINI007 does not receive or hold customer payments for independent sellers’ product sales and therefore does not directly process those product-sale refunds. Customers should contact the relevant seller first regarding a product return or refund. This does not remove any rights customers may have under applicable law.

2. Seller Membership Payments

Membership fees are paid to KINI007 for access to seller membership features. Before purchasing, sellers should review the plan, subscription duration, features, total payable amount, and applicable payment conditions.

3. Membership Refund Requests

Membership refund requests must be submitted with the seller account email, membership plan, payment date, transaction reference, and reason for the request. Each request will be reviewed according to the circumstances, applicable consumer-protection laws, and payment-provider requirements.

Membership fees are not automatically refundable merely because a seller changes their mind, does not use the membership, or allows it to expire, subject to applicable law.

4. Duplicate Payments

If a seller believes they have paid twice for the same membership, they should contact KINI007 with the relevant transaction references. Verified duplicate payments will be reviewed for correction or refund.

5. Failed or Pending Payments

If a payment is deducted but the membership is not activated, the seller should contact KINI007 and provide the payment reference. KINI007 will investigate the payment status before taking appropriate action.

6. Membership Cancellation

Sellers may stop renewing their membership. Cancellation of renewal does not automatically mean that a previously paid membership fee will be refunded, subject to applicable law.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be handled through the applicable payment provider or payment method, subject to its processing rules. Processing time may vary depending on the payment provider.
